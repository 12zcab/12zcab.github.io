2. Snell's Law of Refraction

Overhead rays enter water surface normal $\mathbf{N}$. Rays bend towards normal ($n_2 > n_1$).

3. Ripple Lens Effect

  • Crest = Convex Lens: Converges light $\rightarrow$ Bright projection band.
  • Trough = Concave Lens: Diverges light $\rightarrow$ Dark projection band.
