Ripple Tank: Lens Effect

When overhead light shines through a water wave:

  • Wave Crest: Thicker water layer acts as a Convex Lens $\rightarrow$ Converges light $\rightarrow$ BRIGHT line on screen.
  • Wave Trough: Thinner water layer acts as a Concave Lens $\rightarrow$ Diverges light $\rightarrow$ DARK line on screen.
